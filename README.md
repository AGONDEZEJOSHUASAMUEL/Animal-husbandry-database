**#🐑 Animal-husbandry-database**
Video link on youtube https://youtu.be/YwFT9nQlCeM

🌱 What’s this project about?
This project is all about helping farmers, vets, and researchers keep track of animals in a smarter way. Instead of juggling notebooks, spreadsheets, or scattered records, the database brings everything together — from animal details to feeding — in one organized system.

Think of it as a digital farm diary that never forgets.


**🎯 Why we built it**
To make animal records easy to find (no more flipping through pages).

To keep health history safe and accessible.

To give farmers and managers better insights for decision-making.

To support sustainable farming practices with data-driven choices.

**What’s inside the database**
Here are the main sections you’ll find:

Animals → Who they are: species, breed, age, gender.

Health Records → How they’re doing: medical notes.

Feeding → What they eat: diet plans.

Employees → Who’s in charge: farmer details and ownership.

**Features you’ll love**__
Simple CRUD: Add, update, or remove records without hassle.

Smart search: Find animals by animalID, species, or health status.

Reports: Quick summaries of health trends.

User roles: Different access levels for managers, vets, and herdsmens
